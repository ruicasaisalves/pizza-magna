## How things are cut
Each topping can go on **whole/sliced** or **grated/chopped** (as in the original game).
Exceptions that are never chopped: drizzles and drops (honey, olive oil, balsamic), spreads (Nutella, mayonnaise, sour cream, catupiry) and things that are already small (sweetcorn, pine nuts, potato sticks, chickpeas).

**Chopping rule:** herbs, vegetables (mushrooms included) and cheeses → fine grating (1–2 px strips or crumbs). Meats and sausages → 3–4 px cubes in 3/4 view. Fish and seafood → irregular 4–6 px flakes. Grated pieces cover more area with less weight, spread the flavour evenly and change the cost per portion. Grated cheese is a big loose handful (60×56 px), so 10–12 cover a pizza.

**Weight:** every topping has a weight in grams per whole piece (`GRAMS` in `index.html`); a chopped piece weighs 70% of it and a grated-cheese handful 150%.

## Notes
- Presunto and prosciutto both stay: the Portuguese one matters, but pizza is Italian. Prosciutto is pink, thin and draped; presunto is dark red with veins.
- Portuguese toppings (alheira, farinheira, São Jorge, octopus, turnip greens, quince paste) unlocked by region.
- Luxury (truffle, burrata, salmon, prawns): expensive, loved by gourmets, rare at the suppliers.
