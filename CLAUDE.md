# Pizza Magna

A pizzeria management game in 90s pixel art, for browser and phone. Plain HTML and JS, no build and no dependencies: open `index.html` straight from disk.

## Files
- `index.html`: the whole game (about 1500 lines). **Don't read it whole.** Find the section by its marker with grep and read only that:
  - `= LANGUAGE =`: `LANG` and the `I18N` table (en/pt)
  - `= DATA =`: `DOUGHS`, `SAUCES`, `ING`, `PACKS`, `COMBOS`, `SEG` and `custU()` (what each customer group looks for: students a low price and home toppings `CASA`, families a good deal, gourmets quality, tourists a mix and unusual toppings `EXOTIC`; the price a customer finds fair, `fairPrice()`, comes mostly from appeal, and toppings add value only up to `VALUE_CAP` of cost, so a pizza piled with toppings and sold cheap doesn't take every customer), `THEMES` (contest themes by topping or flavour: classic, sweet, veggie, creative, cheese, spicy, sea, low-cost; names in `I18N` `th.*`) and the economy constants (the goal, `GOAL_MONEY`/`GOAL_REP` for a second shop, is off for now: `GOAL_ON=false` hides it from the header and the intro and skips its announcement, also on the 3DS); `weekly()`: the topping, least wanted topping and combo of the week; `STUDY_COST`, `studyTips()`: the weekly market study in the Shop (tips in `state.tips`)
  - `DEV` (pizzamagna.xyz/dev, `window.PM_DEV` from `dev/index.html`, or `#dev`): for features still being tried out, off on the public site and itch.io; same save for both. Nothing is behind it now: the City, the Bank and the harder reputation went public in 0.03e.
  - Reputation: a customer is happy at `happyAt()` = `HAPPY_BASE` + `HAPPY_STAR` × stars (60 at 1★, 80 at 5★) and a good day's gain shrinks near the top (`repGain()`, `REP_TOP`): a very good menu reaches 5★ in about two months (it took 3–4 weeks); ported to the 3DS (`day.lua`, `HARD_REP=true`).
  - `= PIZZA ANALYSIS =`: `analyze()` (quality, cost, appeal: per customer group, `Q_W` of the quality plus `FIT_W` times how well the pizza fits that group, so a pizza made for a group wins it against a better pizza made for another) and `contestScore()`
  - `= DRAWING`: atlas, `spr()`, rotating pieces (`turned()`), `composePizza()` (pieces are drawn in the order they were placed)
  - `= UI: KITCHEN =` and `-- the pizza bench`: the bench (320 wide, with a title strip on top: tapping it renames the pizza, `renameDraft()`; zoom mode; the floppy next to undo, `quicksave`, puts the pizza on the menu and keeps it on the bench, `savePizza(true)`), the bowls, `addPiece()`, `rotatePiece()`, `GRAMS` and `portion()` (weights)
  - `= UI: DAY =`, `-- simulation` (regulars, about 45%, come back unless the pizza is poor or overpriced; each customer picks among the pizzas within `SLACK` of what they'd accept by a logit on utility, `pickPizza()`/`pickW()`, `PICK_T` wide, so the best pizza for them sells most but not all; `demandCurve()` and the Menu's "Who picks it", `menuShares()`, use the same rule; the day report shows who bought what, `res.bySeg`; "Who picks it" and "Who bought what" show only with Advanced analytics), `-- restaurant room`: the day, the customers and the tiled room (32 px tiles, art and piece layouts from `assets/room.js`); `RZ`: room zoom 1–3 (− / + buttons, two-finger pinch, ctrl+wheel) shared by the day and the room editor, the canvas scrolls inside `.rscroll`
  - `-- room design` (state.room: pieces, seats, atmosphere per customer group, `roomAnalyse()`; a table counts only with a free tile next to it linked to the door: the editor marks the others ⛔, turns the ghost orange before a placement would close a table in, `edBlockedIf()`, and the Day shows a warning), `= UI: ROOM EDITOR =` (Shop › Decorate the room)
  - Staff (`state.staff`, per shop, in `SHOP_KEYS`; old saves' `cooks` count becomes average people in `ensureShops()`): pizzaiolos and waiters, each with a name and two skills from 1 to 5, shown as 3/5 (pizzaiolos: speed and cooking; waiters: speed and charm), and a wage that follows them a little (`wageOf()`, `SALARY` / `WAITER_SALARY`). A cook's speed sets the kitchen's pizzas a day (`capacity()`, `COOK_CAP` at 3/5), a waiter's the customers the room can serve (`serviceCap()`, `WAITER_CAP`); the day serves the smaller of the two; the second skill (`sk`: cooking, charm) adds to each customer's satisfaction (`crewSat` in `simulateDay()`); faster waiters also walk faster in the room. Every day brings three new pizzaiolos and three new waiters to hire (`candidates()`, `state.cands`, shared by all shops; `HIRE_COST`, at most `MAX_STAFF`); the Shop's Team card hires and lets go; with the manager in charge (tier 1) the manager hires the fastest candidate when the kitchen or the room is short. Not ported to the 3DS yet (it still counts pizzaiolos).
  - `-- stock` (pantry by weight: `state.stock`, nothing spoils) and who runs the pizzeria, `state.tier`: 1 the manager runs everything (buys at +25%, sets prices, hires; `TIER_FEE`), 2 a buyer refills the pantry every morning for `restockDays`, 3 the player does everything; the supplier, `state.supplier` (`SUPPLIER`, `ingC()`): 1/2/3 adds quality to every pizza and makes toppings dearer, and no topping is ever locked; `managerMorning()`, `= UI: PANTRY =` (Shop › Pantry), `= DEMAND CURVE =` (the whole menu's profit by one pizza's price; shown in the Menu only with Options › Advanced analytics, `ADV`, `pm-adv` in `localStorage`; by default the Menu shows just quality, cost, margin and sold yesterday)
  - `= UI: HISTORY =`: the sales history page (Menu › Sales history, or from the end-of-day report): `state.hist` keeps the last `HIST_DAYS` (60) days, one record per day written by `finishDay()` (served, street, rival, profit, takings, reputation and the pizzas sold by name)
  - Several shops: the open shop's fields (`SHOP_KEYS`) live at the root of `state`, the others in `state.shops[i].data`; `openShop(i)` swaps them, `curShop()`, `shopName()`, `shopRent()` (`rentOf(i)`: the first shop `RENT`, a bought one 0, a rented one its lot's rent, by neighbourhood and foot traffic only, `RENT_BASE` in `city.js`); a new shop's neighbourhood sets its customer mix (`segMix()`) and its street's foot traffic the number of customers (`trafficMul()`). The header's ‹ › arrows switch shops. Each shop's menu is its own; the Menu offers the other shops' pizzas (the recipe book, `bookPizzas()`). `finishDay()` = `shopDay()` for the watched shop + `otherShopsDay()` (the others in the background with their manager; closed with no menu or seats, still paying rent and wages) + the day's global part; the report adds `shopsTable()`.
  - `= UI: CITY =` (the City tab of the bottom bar, which replaced the Contest tab; the contest opens from the City, `contestBtn()`, on top and on the main square, and its screen goes back to the City): the map from `city.js`; with `CITY_CASH` ($4000) in cash, rent a lot (rent per day, works now) or buy one (no rent); the new shop opens empty (`newShopData()`: no tables, no menu, one oven and one pizzaiolo, the nearest rival, `RIVAL_HOOD`). Each neighbourhood's rival has a strategy (`RSTYLE`, `HOOD_STYLE`: cheap, tourist, family, gourmet; the first shop's is classic). A shop's city panel closes it (rented: the lease ends) or sells it (bought: `SELL_SHARE`, 70% of the price), never the only one (`closeShop()`); its pizzas stay in the recipe book (`state.archive`). The bank (Shop › Bank): borrow up to `BANK_MAX` ($20,000) in steps of $500, $2000 or $5000, `BANK_RATE` (0.5%) of the debt charged every day in `finishDay()`; `state.loan`; the $4000 to open a shop is your own money, `ownMoney()` = cash − loan.
  - `= UI: SHOP =`, `= UI: CONTEST =`, `= MODALS =`, `= RECIPES: LINK, FILE, IMPORT =`, `= SHARE =`
  - `= RENDER + EVENTS =`: `render()` and the button actions (`ACT`), plus `TRACK` (analytics events)
- `music.js`: 8-bit / lo-fi Italian music, synthesised with Web Audio (no sound files). Each tune is text: one note per eighth, `-` holds, `.` is silence. Lead, bass and harmony must have the same number of steps per part. Tunes play in a fixed order ("Next song" in Options): every tune once, as listed in `SONGS`, then from the top (`ORDER`); songs marked `calm: true` (slow, quieter) are in the list like the others, not put in between. The first tune of a visit starts a minute after the first tap (`FIRST_WAIT`); then a new tune starts every 3 minutes, with silence after each one; the volume (`pm-music-vol`, 1–5) defaults to 2. Skipping a tune is in Options; the corner button next to mute (a calendar with ▶) is the next day (`nextday`: opens the doors from any screen, and skips to closing while the day runs). The public-domain classics come from ABC sheet music (abcnotation.com), converted with `tools/abc2music.py`. The on/off preference lives in `localStorage` (`pm-music`), apart from the save.
- `city.js`: the city, Porto Magno: a 6×5 grid of blocks and a river drawn pixel by pixel in code (`CITY.paint()`, `CITY.life()` for cars and walkers), the four neighbourhoods (`HOODS`: customer mix, rent factor, foot traffic) and every lot (`LOTS`: kind, address, traffic, rent, price, condition), all from fixed seeds; `paint(owned)` paints the player's shops as open pizzerias, `LIFE_SPEED` 0: cars and people are parked, and the City screen draws only when something changes (`CV.draw`). Shared by the game's City screen and `docs/drafts/city.html`.
- `assets/ingredients.js`: the toppings atlas. **It is generated** by `tools/build_atlas.py`; never edit it by hand. See `tools/CLAUDE.md`.
- `assets/room.js`: the dining room atlas at 32 px tiles (furniture, decor, kitchen, people) with the layout of each piece. **Generated** by `tools/gen_room.py` (the people come from `tools/pilot_people.py`: 21 customers as `w<look>_<dir>_<0-2>` on foot, standing then two strides played 1, 0, 2, 0, and `p<look>_<dir>` seated, plus the staff, `waiter`, `waiter_1`… and `cook`, `cook_1`…: `WAITERS` and `COOKS` in `pilot_people.py`, counted in `layout.staff` and handed out in turn by the game); never edit it by hand. Used by the game's room (loaded only when the room is first shown, `roomArt()`) and by `docs/drafts/room.html`.
- `assets/icon-*.png` and `manifest.webmanifest`: the game's icon (a pizza slice) for the browser tab, the header button, the phone home screen and a future app. **Generated** by `tools/gen_icon.py`: 32 px drawn pixel by pixel; 180/192/512 cut from a real game pizza.
- `sw.js`: service worker (network first, keeps the last copy for offline play); registered only over http(s). Installing the game: `= INSTALL =` in `index.html` (Options section, and a one-time invitation on phones after the first pizza saved; `pm-install-asked` in `localStorage`).
- `3ds/`: a Nintendo 3DS homebrew port with LÖVE Potion (Lua), with the Kitchen, the Menu, the Day, the Contest and the Shop; `3ds/README.md` explains it. `3ds/game/data.lua` is **generated** from `index.html` by `node tools/export_3ds.js`, and `3ds/game/atlas.lua` plus `3ds/game/gfx/` by `python3 tools/export_3ds.py`. Run both after changing toppings, combos or `I18N`. `3ds/game/pizza.lua` (`analyze()`, `contestScore()`) and `3ds/game/day.lua` (`simulateDay()`, `finishDay()`, contest, shop) are ported by hand: change them too when those change.
- `old/`: earlier models kept in case we go back (the first 16 px room: `old/sprites.js` and `old/room-16px.js`; the first people of the 32 px room: `old/room-first-people.js` and `old/first-people.png`; `old/README.md` says how to go back). Not loaded by the game.
- `itch/`: the kit for the game's itch.io page: cover, banner, background tile, logo and screenshots (generated by `node itch/make_media.js` from the game itself) and `PAGE.md` (colours, tags, description, how to make the upload zip).
- `docs/ingredients.md`: the topping list (✓★ = in the game) and how things are cut.
- `docs/art-packs.md`: art packs considered and the style decision.
- `docs/ideas.md`: ideas on hold.
- `docs/roadmap.md`: what is still missing to make a full tycoon game, and in which order.

## Rules
- **Language of the game:** English by default and Portuguese when the browser is in `pt`; in Options the player can fix English or Português (`pm-lang` in `localStorage`). All visible text comes from `I18N`, with the same key in `en` and `pt`. Everything is migrated (only proper names, like the rival pizzerias and Portuguese dish names, stay as they are); new text must go through `t()`. The news (`state.news`) are saved as text; `newsText()` rewrites them in the current language when shown, so a news line needs a key in `NEWS_KEYS`. Inside `simulateDay()` a local `t` (time) hides `t()`: use its alias `tx()` there.
- **Language of the repo:** documentation (`.md`), commit messages and new code comments in English, so the repo can be shared with English-speaking colleagues. Only game text in `I18N` (and Portuguese names like francesinha or alheira) stays in Portuguese. Reply to the user in the language they write in (they usually write Portuguese, pt-PT).
- **Version:** `VERSION` in `index.html` (near `SAVE_KEY`) is shown at the bottom of Options and written into the save (`state.version`) so bug reports say which build. Every PR that gets merged bumps it one step in that PR: `0.01a`, `0.01b` … `0.01g`, then `0.02a` … `0.02g`, `0.03a` and so on.
- **Saves:** state goes to `localStorage` (`massa-mestra-v1`); pieces are stored compactly (`packPieces`, 5 bytes per piece), the same format as recipe links (`#p=`) and `.pizza` files. No limit on pizzas in the menu; up to 300 pieces per pizza. New fields need a default value, so old saves still open.
- **Analytics:** GoatCounter (`pizzamagna.goatcounter.com`). Buttons worth counting are in `TRACK` and arrive as `ev/<name>` events; a new button worth counting goes there. Never send names or recipes.
- **Fonts:** all are local, in `assets/fonts/` (latin and latin-ext woff2 files from Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License), so the game looks the same offline; no Google Fonts link. Body text is Pixelify Sans (the original font; the player tried VT323, Jersey 10 and Tiny5 and prefers it), but its digits muddle at small sizes (5 looks like S or 8, 2 like Z), so the digits 0-9 come from VT323: `'PM Digits'` (`assets/digits.woff2`, the ten digits only, `unicode-range`) goes first in `--f-body` and in canvas fonts. Titles and buttons Silkscreen; the green LCDs and the Menu figures VT323. Write ordinals as words (segunda, primeiro lugar).
- Don't add dependencies or build steps.
- Keep diffs surgical: every changed line should trace back to the request. Remove only what your own change made unused; flag other dead code, don't delete it unasked.
- Before saying a change works, verify it: `node tools/test_ingredients.js` for toppings, Playwright on `index.html#sandbox` (or the full game) for screens.

## Testing
- The public site (`pizzamagna.xyz`, GitHub Pages from `main`) runs the full game. It used to show only the bench, saved under the bench key; `load()` adopts that save for players who have no full-game save.
- Test mode: `index.html#sandbox` (or `window.PM_SANDBOX=true`) opens only the Kitchen and the Menu, with a separate save.
- `pizzamagna.xyz/dev` still works (`dev/index.html` fetches `../index.html` and adds `<base href="../">`) and is now the same as the site. There is only one copy of the game: never copy `index.html` into `dev/`.
- `node tools/test_ingredients.js [id1,id2] <folder>`: checks sprites, translations, combos and weights, and places pieces on the bench.
- For other screens, use Playwright: `require('playwright')` works without installing anything.

## Tasks with their own procedure
- New toppings or fixing a sprite: use the `novo-lote` skill.
- Changing the sprite generators: read `tools/CLAUDE.md`.
