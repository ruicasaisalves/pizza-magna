# Pizza Magna

A pizzeria management game in 90s pixel art, for browser and phone. Plain HTML and JS, no build and no dependencies: open `index.html` straight from disk.

## Files
- `index.html`: the whole game (about 1500 lines). **Don't read it whole.** Find the section by its marker with grep and read only that:
  - `= LANGUAGE =`: `LANG` and the `I18N` table (en/pt)
  - `= DATA =`: `DOUGHS`, `SAUCES`, `ING`, `PACKS`, `COMBOS`, `SEG` and `custU()` (what each customer group looks for: students a low price and home toppings `CASA`, families a good deal, gourmets quality, tourists a mix and unusual toppings `EXOTIC`; the price a customer finds fair, `fairPrice()`, comes mostly from appeal, and toppings add value only up to `VALUE_CAP` of cost, so a pizza piled with toppings and sold cheap doesn't take every customer), `THEMES` (contest themes by topping or flavour: classic, sweet, veggie, creative, cheese, spicy, sea, low-cost; names in `I18N` `th.*`) and the economy constants (the goal, `GOAL_MONEY`/`GOAL_REP` for a second shop, is off for now: `GOAL_ON=false` hides it from the header and the intro and skips its announcement, also on the 3DS); `weekly()`: the topping, least wanted topping and combo of the week; `STUDY_COST`, `studyTips()`: the weekly market study in the Shop (tips in `state.tips`)
  - `DEV` (pizzamagna.xyz/dev, `window.PM_DEV` from `dev/index.html`, or `#dev`): for features still being tried out, off on the public site and itch.io; same save for both. Nothing is behind it now (the cast-01 characters went public in 0.04k). The City, the Bank and the harder reputation went public in 0.03e.
  - The characters (`assets/cast/`, its `README.md` and `NOTES.md`; cast-01, public since 0.04k): all 21 customers, 9 waiters and 11 standing cooks from their own sheets, folder NN = look NN − 1 (`castCustomer()`, `castWaiter()`, `castCook()`), each once it has decoded (`castSheet()`), else the people of the room atlas; walkers, waiters and cooks drawn 1 px higher with the atlas's foot shadow (`drawPilotFoot()`), seated ones at the atlas layout's place and cut at row 36 from behind (`drawPilotSeated()`), per-frame head nudges `PILOT_DX`; a waiter carries the pizza in his own frames and faces the customer for `PILOT_HANDOFF` (0.3 s) at the table. Options › Characters picks New or Classic (the atlas people, as before 0.04k; `CAST_PREF`, `pm-cast` in `localStorage`); `CAST_ON=false` turns the new ones off for everyone; main before the change is commit 40807a3. Earlier pilots stay in `dev/pilots/` (trio-01, waiter-01, cooks-01-05), not drawn.
  - Guests (`guestLook()`, since 0.04l; cast sheets only, so never with the classic characters; looks only, sales don't change): the wizard (`customers/22`) and the goth (`customers/23`) come in `GUEST_P` (2.5%) of the people each. The VIPs (`VIPS`, nicknames only, never real names) visit our pizzeria from their stars on, once a day every `every` days (shifted by `off`), after `from` of the day: vipspeed (`customers/24`, 3★, every 2 days); captain, redsuit, goldline (`customers/25`–`27`, 4★) and bow, moustache (`customers/28`–`29`, 5★), every 3 days each. Christmas guests (since 0.05a; `customers/30`–`34`), seasonal, only from 1 November to 31 January (`XMAS_ON()`, the player's date): Santa once a day after `XMAS_SANTA` of the day, two elves and a reindeer guest (`XMAS_P`, 3% each) and a green monster hoodie (half that); on the 3DS too (`guestLook()` in `sala.lua`).
  - Reputation: a customer is happy at `happyAt()` = `HAPPY_BASE` + `HAPPY_STAR` × stars (60 at 1★, 80 at 5★) and a good day's gain shrinks near the top (`repGain()`, `REP_TOP`): a very good menu reaches 5★ in about two months (it took 3–4 weeks); ported to the 3DS (`day.lua`, `HARD_REP=true`).
  - `= PIZZA ANALYSIS =`: `analyze()` (quality, cost, appeal: per customer group, `Q_W` of the quality plus `FIT_W` times how well the pizza fits that group, so a pizza made for a group wins it against a better pizza made for another) and `contestScore()`
  - `= DRAWING`: atlas, `spr()`, rotating pieces (`turned()`), `composePizza()` (pieces are drawn in the order they were placed)
  - `= UI: KITCHEN =` and `-- the pizza bench`: the bench (320 wide, with a title strip on top: tapping it renames the pizza, `renameDraft()`; zoom mode; the floppy next to undo, `quicksave`, puts the pizza on the menu and keeps it on the bench, `savePizza(true)`), the bowls, `addPiece()`, `rotatePiece()`, `GRAMS` and `portion()` (weights)
  - `= UI: DAY =`, `-- simulation` (regulars, about 45%, come back unless the pizza is poor or overpriced; each customer picks among the pizzas within `SLACK` of what they'd accept by a logit on utility, `pickPizza()`/`pickW()`, `PICK_T` wide, so the best pizza for them sells most but not all; `demandCurve()` and the Menu's "Who picks it", `menuShares()`, use the same rule; the day report shows who bought what, `res.bySeg`; "Who picks it" and "Who bought what" show only with Advanced analytics), `-- restaurant room`: the day, the customers and the tiled room (32 px tiles, art and piece layouts from `assets/room.js`); customers walking out wear a yellow face with a see-through ring, happy (green), neutral (yellow: served, within `MEH_BAND` of `happyAt()`) or unhappy (red: further below, or gave up for the queue, a table or stock); `faceArt()`, `faceOf()`, counted in the day report (`facesHTML()`, which replaced the happy / disappointed row) and the history (`histFaces()`); with Advanced analytics only, a small icon beside the face says why (`e.why` from `simulateDay()`: pizza, price, staff, room, or a clock for those who gave up; `whyArt()`) for 10% of the happy, 30% of the neutral and 40% of the unhappy (`WHY_P`); the reputation still counts neutral and unhappy alike (the 3DS shows the faces too: in its room, its day report and its sales history, `res.meh`/`res.sad` and the history's `f` in `day.lua`); while the day runs, Pause stops its clock (`anim.paused`, the room is still drawn, so zoom and scroll work; a speed button resumes); `RZ`: room zoom 1–3 (− / + buttons, two-finger pinch, ctrl+wheel) shared by the day and the room editor, the canvas scrolls inside `.rscroll`
  - `-- room design` (state.room: pieces, seats, atmosphere per customer group, `roomAnalyse()`; Christmas since 0.04z: decor pieces (the tree, `arvore`, whose lights twinkle, a snowman, a poinsettia, a gift pile), wall pieces (a wreath, stockings, a snowy window) and the lights, `luzes`, a wall item that isn't put in a slot but spans the whole back wall, `room.lights`, bought or sold with one tap in the editor's wall tab; a table counts only with a free tile next to it linked to the door: the editor marks the others ⛔, turns the ghost orange before a placement would close a table in, `edBlockedIf()`, and the Day shows a warning), `= UI: ROOM EDITOR =` (Shop › Decorate the room)
  - Staff (`state.staff`, per shop, in `SHOP_KEYS`; old saves' `cooks` count becomes average people in `ensureShops()`): pizzaiolos and waiters, each with a name (a woman's name, `STAFF_WOMEN`, gets one of the women's figures, `staffV()`; with the new characters `CAST_STAFF`, else the atlas's `STAFF_LOOKS`) and two skills from 1 to 5, shown as 3/5 (pizzaiolos: speed and cooking; waiters: speed and charm), and a wage that follows them a little (`wageOf()`, `SALARY` / `WAITER_SALARY`). A cook's speed sets the kitchen's pizzas a day (`capacity()`, `COOK_CAP` at 3/5), a waiter's the customers the room can serve (`serviceCap()`, `WAITER_CAP`); the day serves the smaller of the two; the second skill (`sk`: cooking, charm) adds to each customer's satisfaction (`crewSat` in `simulateDay()`); faster waiters also walk faster in the room. Every day brings three new pizzaiolos and three new waiters to hire (`candidates()`, `state.cands`, shared by all shops; `HIRE_COST`, at most `MAX_STAFF`); the Shop's Team card hires and lets go; with the manager in charge (tier 1) the manager hires when recoverable sales cover the wage and a fourteenth of the hiring fee per day. Ported to the 3DS (`day.lua`: `G.capacity`, `G.serviceCap`, `G.candidates`, `crewSat`; Shop › Team and Looking for work today), without the manager.
  - `-- stock` (pantry by weight: `state.stock`, nothing spoils) and who runs the pizzeria, `state.tier`: 1 the manager runs everything (buys at +25%, sets prices, hires; `TIER_FEE`), 2 a buyer refills the pantry every morning for `restockDays`, 3 the player does everything; the supplier, `state.supplier` (`SUPPLIER`, `ingC()`): 1/2/3 adds quality to every pizza and makes toppings dearer, and no topping is ever locked; `managerMorning()`, `= UI: PANTRY =` (Shop › Pantry), `= DEMAND CURVE =` (the whole menu's profit by one pizza's price; shown in the Menu only with Options › Advanced analytics, `ADV`, `pm-adv` in `localStorage`; by default the Menu shows just quality, cost, margin and sold yesterday)
  - `= UI: HISTORY =`: the sales history page (Menu › Sales history, or from the end-of-day report): `state.hist` keeps the last `HIST_DAYS` (60) days, one record per day written by `finishDay()` (served, street, rival, profit, takings, reputation, how customers left `f` = [happy, neutral, unhappy] and the pizzas sold by name)
  - Several shops: the open shop's fields (`SHOP_KEYS`) live at the root of `state`, the others in `state.shops[i].data`; `openShop(i)` swaps them, `curShop()`, `shopName()`, `shopRent()` (`rentOf(i)`: the first shop `RENT`, a bought one 0, a rented one its lot's rent, by neighbourhood and foot traffic only, `RENT_BASE` in `city.js`); a new shop's neighbourhood sets its customer mix (`segMix()`) and its street's foot traffic the number of customers (`trafficMul()`). The header's ‹ › arrows switch shops. Each shop's menu is its own; the Menu offers the other shops' pizzas (the recipe book, `bookPizzas()`). `finishDay()` = `shopDay()` for the watched shop + `otherShopsDay()` (the others in the background with their manager; closed with no menu or seats, still paying rent and wages) + the day's global part; the report adds `shopsTable()`.
  - `= UI: CITY =` (the City tab of the bottom bar, which replaced the Contest tab; the contest opens from the City, `contestBtn()`, on top and on the main square, and its screen goes back to the City): the map from `city.js`; with `CITY_CASH` ($4000) in cash, rent a lot (rent per day, works now) or buy one (no rent); the new shop opens empty (`newShopData()`: no tables, no menu, one oven and one pizzaiolo, the nearest rival, `RIVAL_HOOD`). Each neighbourhood's rival has a strategy (`RSTYLE`, `HOOD_STYLE`: cheap, tourist, family, gourmet; the first shop's is classic). A shop's city panel closes it (rented: the lease ends) or sells it (bought: `SELL_SHARE`, 70% of the price), never the only one (`closeShop()`); its pizzas stay in the recipe book (`state.archive`). The bank (Shop › Bank): borrow up to `BANK_MAX` ($20,000) in steps of $500, $2000 or $5000, `BANK_RATE` (0.5%) of the debt charged every day in `finishDay()`; `state.loan`; the $4000 to open a shop is your own money, `ownMoney()` = cash − loan.
  - The pizzeria's symbol (`state.logo`, after `= CHEF =`): picked in the chef and pizzeria window (at the start, or the header's name button): the game's slice (none), one of 15 ready-made symbols (`LOGOS`: 32×32 pixel art from canvas drawings and `assets/menu-art.js`, by id: pizza, slice, tomato, four chefs (men and women with light and dark skin), a cat, a dog, chef's hat, peel and rolling pin, wood oven, striped awning, Italian shield, the pizzeria's initial on a coin; `presetLogo()`) or a photo, cut square and shrunk to `LOGO_PX` (a data: URL in the save, `photoLogo()`); shown in the header and, on the chef's own pizzas, beside the signature on the share card (`shareCard()`, which also shows the pizza's quality as a gold pixel star and the score on the dough line, `cardStar()`).
  - `= PAGE BACKGROUND =`: `pageBg()` picks one of three pixel-art walls at random each time the game opens (wood planks with grain and nails, brick, a dimmed red and white check cloth), drawn in canvas at 1x and tripled into body's background; the CSS planks on `body` stay as the fallback.
  - `= UI: SHOP =`, `= UI: CONTEST =`, `= MODALS =`, `= RECIPES: LINK, FILE, IMPORT =`, `= SHARE =`
  - `= RENDER + EVENTS =`: `render()` and the button actions (`ACT`), plus `TRACK` (analytics events)
- `music.js`: 8-bit / lo-fi Italian music, synthesised with Web Audio (no sound files). Each tune is text: one note per eighth, `-` holds, `.` is silence. Lead, bass and harmony must have the same number of steps per part. Twelve original lo-fi tunes (`lofi: true`, six since 0.04t, six more since 0.04v) add an arpeggio line (`arp`) and hold chords in `harm` (`F3+A3+C4`); they play with a rounder lead, a soft triangle pad, quieter drums and a warmer 1500 Hz filter. Tunes play in a random order ("Next song" in Options) that alternates lo-fi and Italian (each kind shuffled apart, starting with either; when one kind runs out, the rest of the other follow): every tune once each, then a new deal (`ORDER`, `deal()`; never the same tune twice in a row); songs marked `calm: true` (slow, quieter) are in the list like the others, not put in between. Christmas tunes (`xmas: true`, since 0.05c: four public-domain carols, Jingle Bells, Deck the Halls, We Wish You a Merry Christmas and Silent Night, the last one lo-fi, plus an original swing crooner tune, Neve sulla Pizzeria, also lo-fi) are dealt only from 1 November to 31 January (`XMAS_SEASON()`, the player's date, as the Christmas guests); the 3DS has them too (`xmas` in `musicdata.lua`, `deal()` in `musica.lua`). The first tune of a visit starts a minute after the first tap (`FIRST_WAIT`); then a new tune starts every 3 minutes, with silence after each one; the volume (`pm-music-vol`, 1–5) defaults to 2. Skipping a tune is in Options; the corner button next to mute (a calendar with ▶) is the next day (`nextday`: opens the doors from any screen, and skips to closing while the day runs). The public-domain classics come from ABC sheet music (abcnotation.com), converted with `tools/abc2music.py`. The on/off preference lives in `localStorage` (`pm-music`), apart from the save. The 3DS plays the same tunes recorded as OGG (`3ds/game/music/`, `musicdata.lua`, **generated** by `node tools/export_3ds_music.js`, which copies the voices of `music.js`: change both together) with the same order rules (`3ds/game/musica.lua`; Options › Music on the 3DS).
- `city.js`: the city, Porto Magno: a 6×5 grid of blocks and a river drawn pixel by pixel in code (`CITY.paint()`, `CITY.life()` for cars and walkers), the four neighbourhoods (`HOODS`: customer mix, rent factor, foot traffic) and every lot (`LOTS`: kind, address, traffic, rent, price, condition), all from fixed seeds; `paint(owned)` paints the player's shops as open pizzerias, `LIFE_SPEED` 0: cars and people are parked, and the City screen draws only when something changes (`CV.draw`). Shared by the game's City screen and `docs/drafts/city.html`; the 3DS gets its lots in `data.lua` (`tools/export_3ds.js`) and its map as `3ds/game/gfx/city.png` (`node tools/export_3ds_city.js`, run it after changing `city.js`).
- `assets/ingredients.js`: the toppings atlas. **It is generated** by `tools/build_atlas.py`; never edit it by hand. See `tools/CLAUDE.md`.
- `assets/room.js`: the dining room atlas at 32 px tiles (furniture, decor, kitchen, people) with the layout of each piece. **Generated** by `tools/gen_room.py` (the people come from `tools/pilot_people.py`: 21 customers as `w<look>_<dir>_<0-2>` on foot, standing then two strides played 1, 0, 2, 0, and `p<look>_<dir>` seated, plus the staff, `waiter`, `waiter_1`… and `cook`, `cook_1`…: `WAITERS` and `COOKS` in `pilot_people.py`, counted in `layout.staff` and handed out in turn by the game); never edit it by hand. Used by the game's room (loaded only when the room is first shown, `roomArt()`) and by `docs/drafts/room.html`.
- `assets/icon-*.png` and `manifest.webmanifest`: the game's icon (a pizza slice) for the browser tab, the header button, the phone home screen and a future app. **Generated** by `tools/gen_icon.py`: 32 px drawn pixel by pixel; 180/192/512 cut from a real game pizza.
- `sw.js`: service worker (network first, keeps the last copy for offline play); registered only over http(s). Installing the game: `= INSTALL =` in `index.html` (Options section, and a one-time invitation on phones after the first pizza saved; `pm-install-asked` in `localStorage`).
- `3ds/`: a Nintendo 3DS homebrew port with LÖVE Potion (Lua), with the Kitchen, the Menu, the Day (Y: the sales history), the Contest, the Shop (with the staff and the bank) and the City (several shops; `day.lua`: `G.openShop`, `G.otherShopsDay`, `G.openLot`, rivals' `RSTYLE`) and the room editor (Shop › Decorate the room, since 0.05c: the catalogue on the top screen, the room on the touch screen; `S.room` per shop, `roomlib.lua` ports the room's rules and atmosphere, `G.roomStats()` in `day.lua`); no pantry, manager or pizzeria symbol (a new shop gets the starter room and a copy of the open shop's menu); `3ds/README.md` explains it. Since 0.05d its text is a bitmap font, Nunito at 12–28 px (`3ds/game/bfont.lua`; `gfx/font.png` and `fontdata.lua` **generated** by `node tools/export_3ds_font.js` from `tools/fonts/nunito.ttf`), L/R change screens, START opens Options (language, music, quit), the Kitchen's picker is a grid of icons beside the pizza (dough and sauce as its last groups, D-pad to move), the room editor drags pieces, and saves go to `sdmc:/3ds/save/` (the `.cia`'s RomFS is read-only). ## Rules
- **Language of the game:** English by default and Portuguese when the browser is in `pt`; in Options the player can fix English or Português (`pm-lang` in `localStorage`). All visible text comes from `I18N`, with the same key in `en` and `pt`. Everything is migrated (only proper names, like the rival pizzerias and Portuguese dish names, stay as they are); new text must go through `t()`. The news (`state.news`) are saved as text; `newsText()` rewrites them in the current language when shown, so a news line needs a key in `NEWS_KEYS`. Inside `simulateDay()` a local `t` (time) hides `t()`: use its alias `tx()` there.
- **Language of the repo:** documentation (`.md`), commit messages and new code comments in English, so the repo can be shared with English-speaking colleagues. Only game text in `I18N` (and Portuguese names like francesinha or alheira) stays in Portuguese. Reply to the user in the language they write in (they usually write Portuguese, pt-PT).
- **Version:** `VERSION` in `index.html` (near `SAVE_KEY`) is shown at the bottom of Options and written into the save (`state.version`) so bug reports say which build. Every PR that gets merged bumps it one step in that PR: `0.01a`, `0.01b` … `0.01g`, then `0.02a` … `0.02g`, `0.03a` and so on.
- **Saves:** state goes to `localStorage` (`massa-mestra-v1`); pieces are stored compactly (`packPieces`, 5 bytes per piece), the same format as recipe links (`#p=`) and `.pizza` files. No limit on pizzas in the menu; up to 300 pieces per pizza. New fields need a default value, so old saves still open.
- **Analytics:** GoatCounter (`pizzamagna.goatcounter.com`). Buttons worth counting are in `TRACK` and arrive as `ev/<name>` events; a new button worth counting goes there. Never send names or recipes.
- **Fonts:** all are local, in `assets/fonts/` (latin and latin-ext woff2 files from Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License), so the game looks the same offline; no Google Fonts link. Body text is Pixelify Sans (the original font; the player tried VT323, Jersey 10 and Tiny5 and prefers it), but its digits muddle at small sizes (5 looks like S or 8, 2 like Z), so the digits 0-9 come from VT323: `'PM Digits'` (`assets/digits.woff2`, the ten digits only, `unicode-range`) goes first in `--f-body` and in canvas fonts. Titles and buttons Silkscreen; the green LCDs and the Menu figures VT323. Write ordinals as words (segunda, primeiro lugar).
- Don't add dependencies or build steps.
- Keep diffs surgical: every changed line should trace back to the request. Remove only what your own change made unused; flag other dead code, don't delete it unasked.
- Before saying a change works, verify it: `node tools/test_ingredients.js` for toppings, Playwright on `index.html#sandbox` (or the full game) for screens.

## Testing
- The public site (`pizzamagna.xyz`, GitHub Pages from `main`) runs the full game. It used to show only the bench, saved under the bench key; `load()` adopts that save for players who have no full-game save.
- Test mode: `index.html#sandbox` (or `window.PM_SANDBOX=true`) opens only the Kitchen and the Menu, with a separate save.
- `pizzamagna.xyz/dev` still works (`dev/index.html` fetches `../index.html` and adds `<base href="../">`) and is now the same as the site. There is only one copy of the game: never copy `index.html` into `dev/`.
- `node tools/test_ingredients.js [id1,id2] <folder>`: checks sprites, translations, combos and weights, and places pieces on the bench.
- For other screens, use Playwright: `require('playwright')` works without installing anything.

## Tasks with their own procedure
- New toppings or fixing a sprite: use the `novo-lote` skill.
- Changing the sprite generators: read `tools/CLAUDE.md`.
