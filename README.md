# Pizza Magna

A pizzeria management game for browser and phone, in 90s pixel-art style.

Live: https://www.pizzamagna.xyz

## How to play

Open `index.html` in a browser. No install and no server needed.

- **Kitchen:** invent pizzas on a marble bench (a 320×200 logical screen).
  - Each topping goes on whole or grated/chopped. Grated pieces cost and weigh less per portion and spread the flavour; grated cheese comes as a big loose handful, so 10–12 cover a pizza.
  - Some pieces rotate in quarter turns, always the same image turned. Round pieces don't rotate; symmetric ones like bacon have 2 positions (lying, standing); pieces with a front, like a bunch of asparagus, have 4. Rotate with a right-click on the piece or with the **Rotate** button (phone), which turns the last piece placed or the next one.
  - **Zoom** shows only the pizza, as wide as the screen, with the controls as text below (made for phones).
  - Quality depends on amount, spread and combos (110 to discover). The bench shows the suggested price, the number of pieces and the pizza's weight (up to 300 pieces).
- **Menu:** as many pizzas as you like, with adjustable prices, quality, cost, margin and yesterday's sales. Options › Advanced analytics adds, under each price, a chart of the profit a day by price and who picks each pizza. **Sales history** shows the last 60 days.
- **Sharing:** "Download photo" saves a picture card of the pizza. "Share recipe" makes a link (`#p=…`) or a `.pizza` file with every piece in place, signed by the original chef; "Import pizza" opens one.
- **Open:** watch the dining room from above. Customers (students, families, tourists, gourmets) choose between you and the rival next door. The people are pixel-art characters (Options › Characters goes back to the classic ones); now and then a wizard or a goth walks in, and VIPs visit once your pizzeria reaches 3, 4 and 5 stars.
- **Shop:** ovens, pizzaiolos, flyers, the supplier (three quality levels: better toppings, dearer; every topping is always available), and **Decorate the room**: buy and place tables, decor, wall pieces, floor and walls. Tables give the seats for the lunch and dinner rush; each style draws the customers who like it. **Who runs the pizzeria**: the manager (buys, sets prices and hires, for a daily fee), a buyer who refills the pantry every morning, or you; the more you run yourself, the more you earn. **Pantry**: toppings by the day, cheaper in bulk; nothing spoils. **Team**: pizzaiolos and waiters with a name, two skills and a wage; new people look for work every day. **Bank**: borrow up to $20,000, with interest every day.
- **City:** the map of Porto Magno, with four neighbourhoods, each with its own customers, rents and rival. With $4,000 of your own money, rent or buy another shop; each shop has its own menu, room and team, and the header arrows switch between them.
- **Contest:** every 5 days there is a themed contest, opened from the City.

Your pizzeria has a name and a symbol (a ready-made one or a photo), shown in the header and on the pizzas you share. The page behind the screens is one of three pixel-art walls, picked at random each time the game opens.

The public site (GitHub Pages from `main`) runs the full game. `index.html#sandbox` opens only the Kitchen and the Menu, with a separate save, for testing. https://www.pizzamagna.xyz/dev loads the same `index.html` and is kept for old links.

## Technology

Plain HTML and JavaScript, no dependencies and no build step.

- `index.html`: the game, interface and simulation.
- `assets/ingredients.js`: atlas with the toppings, doughs, sauces, bench and bowl in detailed pixel art. Generated by `python3 tools/build_atlas.py` from `tools/gen_ingredients*.py`; never edit it by hand.
- `tools/test_ingredients.js`: tests the toppings in the browser (sprites, translations, combos, rotation, weights). `node tools/test_ingredients.js id1,id2 <folder>`.
- `.claude/skills/novo-lote/`: the procedure for adding a batch of toppings, loaded only when a new batch is asked for.
- `music.js`: 8-bit / lo-fi Italian music, synthesised with Web Audio. Original tunes in the style of Neapolitan classics, plus public-domain classics (La donna è mobile, Santa Lucia, 'O sole mio, Tarantella napoletana) converted from ABC sheet music by `tools/abc2music.py`. They play in a fixed order; the top-right button mutes them.
- `assets/room.js`: the room pixel art (floor, walls, furniture, kitchen, and the classic customers, waiters and cooks) at 32 px per tile, with the layout of each piece; generated by `python3 tools/gen_room.py`. The game's room and room editor use it. The first 16 px model (`sprites.js`) is kept in `old/`.
- `assets/cast/`: the characters drawn in the room: 21 customers, 4 waiters, 5 cooks, plus the guests and VIPs, each from its own sprite sheet (see its `README.md`).
- `city.js`: the city of Porto Magno, drawn pixel by pixel in code, with its neighbourhoods and lots.
- `sw.js` and `manifest.webmanifest`: offline play and installing the game on a phone.
- `3ds/`: a Nintendo 3DS homebrew port (LÖVE Potion); see `3ds/README.md`.
- `itch/`: the kit for the itch.io page (pictures, GIFs, description); see `itch/PAGE.md`.

The dining room is a 20×17 tile map. Customers and staff find their way between tables with breadth-first search (BFS). The game saves to the browser's `localStorage`. Visits and button events are counted with GoatCounter (no names or recipes are sent).

## Language

The game is in **English by default** and switches to **Portuguese** when the browser or phone language is Portuguese (`pt`, `pt-PT`, `pt-BR`); the player can also pick it in Options. All visible text comes from a translation table (`I18N` in `index.html`), never written straight into the code, and every screen is translated.
